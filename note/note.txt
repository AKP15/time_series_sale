Calculate Seasonal Index
Seasonal Index = Average sales for that month ÷ Overall monthly average
Interpretation:
Index > 1 → month tends to be above average
Index < 1 → month tends to be below average
Index = 1 → approximately average

Business interpretation
December is the strongest seasonal month.
An index of 1.325 means December's average sales are about 32.5% above the overall monthly average.
September is the weakest.
Its index of 0.825 means sales are about 17.5% below the overall monthly average.
The pattern looks roughly like:
Jan ↓ → Feb ↓ → Mar–Jun ↑ → Jul–Oct ↓ → Nov ↑ →( Dec )

Moving Average
7-day moving average

Growth rate
Month-over-Month (MoM) Growth.
January: NaN is correct because there is no previous month to compare.
February: sales were almost unchanged → -0.02%
March: strong growth → +23.50%
April: almost unchanged → -0.13%
May: moderate growth → +5.16%
June: sales declined → -3.45%
July: larger decline → -9.78%
Important analyst idea
MoM growth tells you the direction and speed of change, while the moving average tells you the underlying trend.

Business takeaway
The 7-day moving-average forecast is useful for estimating the general short-term sales level, but it is less effective during sudden sales changes because it reacts after the change occurs.

The moving-average baseline captures the overall sales trend but struggles with sudden events and seasonal transitions. The largest forecast errors occurred during exceptional negative-sales days and the beginning of January, when sales were substantially lower than the preceding period.
